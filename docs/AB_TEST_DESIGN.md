# A/B Test Design: "Your usual?" one-tap re-order widget

## Hypothesis

Showing new customers a one-tap re-order of their last order, at the time of day they usually order, increases the
share who place their next order within 30 days. Rationale: real transaction data shows customers become much
more likely to return with each order they place (habit ladder: 41% after the first, 70% after the third), and
customers whose second order comes within 30 days reach a third order far more often (90% vs 66%).

## Design

| Element | Choice |
|---|---|
| Population | Customers who have placed 1 or 2 orders (before the habit forms) |
| Unit of randomisation | User, 50/50 split, assignment fixed for the whole test |
| Control | Current home screen |
| Treatment | "Your usual?" card on the home screen at the user's typical order hour, plus one contextual push a week |
| Primary metric | 30-day re-order rate: share of users placing another order within 30 days of entering the test |
| Secondary metrics | Orders per user in 30 days; share of users reaching their 3rd order; AOV |
| Guardrails (must not worsen) | Order cancellation rate; post-order CSAT; discount spend per order; push opt-out rate; app crash rate |

## Sample size

Two-sided test, 5% significance, 80% power. The baseline 30-day re-order rate is an **assumption**, since
Swiggy does not publish it. The full grid is in `analysis/outputs/ab_test_sample_size.csv` (generated by
`analysis/ab_test_sample_size.py`).

| Baseline re-order rate | Detect +2 pts | Detect +3 pts |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 25% | 7,550 per arm | 3,397 per arm |
| 30% | **8,394 per arm** | 3,763 per arm |
| 35% | 9,041 per arm | 4,042 per arm |
| 40% | 9,493 per arm | 4,234 per arm |

At a 30% baseline, detecting a 2-point lift needs about **16,800 eligible users in total**. Each user must be
observed for 30 days, so the test runs for the enrolment period plus 30 days. At platform scale, enrolment takes
days, so the 30-day observation window sets the duration.

## Decision rule

- **Ship** if the primary metric improves by at least 2 points at 95% confidence and no guardrail worsens
  significantly.
- **Iterate** if the lift is positive but below 2 points, or a guardrail moves the wrong way.
- **Stop** if the effect is flat or negative.

Decide once, at the planned end date. Checking results daily and stopping at the first significant reading
inflates false positives.

## Pitfalls to watch

- **Novelty effect:** new UI gets extra taps at first. Compare the first and second halves of the window.
- **Contamination:** users on two devices must keep one assignment, so randomise on account ID, not device.
- **Segment mix:** report results for discount-led and full-price first orders separately, since the case
  study expects them to behave differently.
